# Cal.com Companion

Cal.com's mobile app for iOS and Android. People who take meetings through Cal.com use it to manage their bookings, links (event types: the pages people book them through), Cal Events (hosted events with guests), availability schedules and their profile.

- Runs use the production Cal.com site with a dedicated test account. Only create, change or delete items whose name starts with "autosana-". Leave everything else as it is.
- The tabs are Bookings, Links, Events, Availability and More. On iOS each tab has a large title with its name. On Android every tab shares one header, the Cal.com logo with the profile avatar, and the tab bar shows which tab is open.
- The profile sheet opens from the avatar or profile button in a tab's header.
- Sign-in happens in an in-app browser: app.cal.com for the United States region, app.eu.cal.com for the European Union region (app.cal.eu redirects there).
- The same flows run on iOS and Android, where controls look different. Use whatever the platform shows to reach the goal.
